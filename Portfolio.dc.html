About

Me, in a nutshell.

I've been fascinated by technology for as long as I can remember. It started with the first computer my father brought home — which I promptly took apart, piece by piece.

Reassembling machines from whatever parts I could find pulled me into networking and OS administration. Then a friend showed me Macromedia Flash 4 — and that lit the spark. Websites, interactive games, and everything since.

Fifteen years on, I lead as well as build. As technical lead at Goalsetter — and lead developer at MediaMonks before that — I've guided teams of up to 12 engineers: setting code standards, mentoring developers, and turning business requirements into products that ship.
